Minimig/AGA includes work by Dennis van Weeren and contributors (GPLv3+).
TG68K includes work by Tobias Gubener (LGPLv3+).
Upstream source headers and notices remain in upstream/ without modification.
fpga/hid.v is generated from upstream/src/misc/hid.v with FIFO availability and
reset-initialization additions. fpga/serializer.sv is a VDAC4 Gowin adapter.
fpga/nanomig.v and fpga/Amber.v are generated local RGB888 adaptations of
upstream/src/nanomig.v and upstream/src/minimig/Amber.v. Original notices are kept.

Common board modules (next_qspi, cp_spi, next_cp, next_evo_reset, fb_sdspi) and
ESP host ABI/PS2 translation files are copied from the existing VDAC4 project.
Their original source headers and licenses apply; fb_sdspi is Apache-2.0.
The GPL text in COPYING is copied from the adjacent VDAC4 NES core.

The user's Kickstart ROM is an external input, not part of the adapter source
or the release binary packages and is not published here.
